## Prompt 001 — Neon Desk Flow

**Use case:** coding, deep work, study

**Style field**
```text
Instrumental calm synthwave for coding and deep work, soft retro analog synth pads, warm rounded bass arpeggio, muted electronic drums, gentle sidechain pulse, subtle glassy keys, neon night desk atmosphere, 84 BPM, smooth modern mix with tape warmth. Stable low-to-medium energy, gradual gentle progression, loopable and non-distracting, no vocals, no lyrics.
```

**Lyrics / structure field**
```text
[Instrumental]
[Intro: warm synth pad fades in, soft filtered arpeggio begins]
[Section A: muted kick, gentle snare, rounded bass arpeggio, minimal chords]
[Section B: subtle glassy key texture enters, no busy lead melody]
[Interlude: drums soften, pad and arpeggio continue steadily]
[Section A reprise: full calm groove returns, smooth and focused]
[Outro: slow filter fade, unresolved enough to loop]
[End]
```

**Exclude styles**
```text
vocals, lyrics, rap, spoken word, harsh drums, aggressive drops, distorted leads, busy solo melody, sudden loud transitions, chaotic percussion, dark horror mood
```

**Notes**
- Intended title: `Neon Desk Flow — Calm Synthwave for Coding`
- Keep if: groove feels steady, not too catchy/distracting, good for 30+ minutes looped.
- Reject if: lead melody dominates or drums become club-like.

## Keep / reject checklist after generation

Keep a generated track if:

- It stays instrumental and vocal-free.
- It has no sudden loud drops or distracting lead solos.
- It can play quietly behind work/study/café ambience.
- The loop or ending feels extendable.
- It matches one clear YouTube packaging angle.

Reject or regenerate if:

- Suno adds vocals, humming, or spoken phrases.
- The drums become too club/EDM/aggressive.
- The melody is too catchy or busy for focus.
- It sounds too dark, tense, or cinematic for calm work.
- It uses obvious copyrighted/franchise-style cues.
