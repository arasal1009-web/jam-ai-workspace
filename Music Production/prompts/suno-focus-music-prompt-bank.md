These are starter prompts for manual testing after niche research. Do not spend credits until Jude chooses which lanes to test.

## 1. Rainy Night Study Café — Lo-fi / Soft Jazz

**Style**
```text
Instrumental rainy night café lo-fi, soft jazz chords, warm electric piano, brushed drums, subtle upright bass, vinyl warmth, distant rain texture, 74 BPM, calm and loopable. Designed for studying and quiet work, no vocals, no lyrics, gentle dynamics with no sudden drops.
```

**Structure**
```text
[Instrumental]
[Intro: rain ambience and soft electric piano]
[Section A: mellow lo-fi groove, brushed drums]
[Section B: gentle jazz chord variation, minimal melody]
[Interlude: rain and soft bass only]
[Section A reprise: warm and steady]
[Outro: soft fade into rain]
[End]
```

**Exclude**
```text
vocals, lyrics, rap, spoken word, harsh drums, aggressive drops, sudden loud transitions, busy lead melody
```
