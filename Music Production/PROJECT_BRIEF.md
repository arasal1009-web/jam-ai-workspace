# Music Production Project Brief

## Why this exists

Jude receives 2,500 Suno AI credits every month and wants a system to maximize their value instead of letting them go unused.

The practical first business/content direction is YouTube background music for students studying or people doing focused work. The broader creative direction includes OST-style music, movie background music, and spontaneous music creation when Jude is inspired.

## Strategic goals

1. Turn monthly Suno credits into a catalog of usable tracks.
2. Prioritize YouTube niches where demand is visible but competition is not overwhelmingly high.
3. Produce repeatable prompt systems for focus/study/work music.
4. Maintain optional creative branches for OST/cinematic cues.
5. Build assets that can later support YouTube playlist packaging, thumbnails, descriptions, and upload schedules.

## Audience hypotheses

| Audience | Need | Music traits |
|---|---|---|
| Students | long, non-distracting study support | low vocals, steady BPM, calm texture, loopable sections |
| Remote workers/coders | deep focus and flow | subtle rhythm, minimal melodic intrusion, stable mood |
| Writers/creatives | atmosphere and emotion | cinematic ambience, fantasy/noir/dark academia variants |
| Filmmakers/content creators | background cues | clearly tagged moods, scene-based cue names, licensing clarity |

## Initial content lanes

1. **Deep Focus / Study** — lofi, ambient, soft piano, synth pads, rain/noise textures.
2. **Coding / Work Flow** — calm synthwave, downtempo electronic, minimal beats.
3. **Dark Academia / Library** — piano, strings, tape warmth, rain, page-turn ambience.
4. **Cozy Fantasy / Writing** — tavern, forest, magical study room, warm acoustic textures.
5. **Cinematic OST Cues** — emotional, suspense, discovery, farewell, victory, transformation.
6. **Filipino/Philippine-inspired ambience** — only with respectful, researched framing; avoid claims or stereotypes.

## Success metrics

For research reports:

- niches ranked with evidence, not guesses;
- clear demand/competition scoring;
- recommendation of what to generate first;
- prompt packs ready for Suno.

For monthly production:

- credits are allocated before the month ends;
- generated tracks are logged with prompt, style, result notes, and keep/reject status;
- best tracks are grouped into playlist concepts.

## Known unknowns

- Suno's current credit cost per generation must be verified from Jude's account or current Suno plan details before calculating exact monthly generation counts.
- YouTube competition metrics require current data collection; initial niche ideas are hypotheses until researched.
