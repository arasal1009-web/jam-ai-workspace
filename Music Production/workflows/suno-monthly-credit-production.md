# Suno Monthly Credit Production Workflow

**Purpose:** Help Jude use his monthly Suno credits intentionally while building a reusable catalog of focus/study/work background music and creative OST cues.

## Inputs

- Available Suno credits this month: default target `2,500`, confirm in account.
- Current credit cost per generation/model: verify from Suno account before exact planning.
- Selected niches from the YouTube Music Niche Researcher.
- Jude's creative priorities for the month.

## Monthly planning steps

1. Verify available credits and credit cost per generation.
2. Choose 3–5 production lanes:
   - 2 proven/safer focus lanes;
   - 1–2 lower-competition experiments;
   - 1 creative/OST lane.
3. Allocate credits:
   - 40% proven focus/study niches;
   - 25% niche experiments;
   - 20% OST/cinematic cues;
   - 10% extend/iterate best tracks;
   - 5% wildcards.
4. Generate prompt packs before opening Suno.
5. Jude manually generates tracks in Suno.
6. Log every promising track with prompt, seed/settings if available, notes, and intended playlist.
7. Group keepers into playlist concepts.
8. Draft YouTube packaging only after tracks are selected.

## Quality checklist for focus/study music

- Mostly instrumental or fully instrumental.
- No sudden loud transitions.
- No distracting lead vocal/lyrics.
- Stable tempo and energy.
- Loopable or extendable.
- Useful for at least 20–60 minutes when compiled.
- Clear mood and playlist fit.

## YouTube packaging checklist

- Playlist concept/title.
- Track order.
- Description draft.
- Tags/keywords draft.
- Thumbnail concept draft.
- Licensing/commercial-use check based on Jude's Suno plan.
- Publishing approval from Jude.

## Approval gates

No automated credit use, account access, upload, schedule, monetization, or public release without Jude's explicit approval.
